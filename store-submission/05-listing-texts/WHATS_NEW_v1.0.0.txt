【Chrome Web Store 更新說明 · v1.0.0 Official Release】

============================================================
English (Release Notes / Update Summary)
============================================================
v1.0.0 Official Stable Release:
• Stall Auto-Recovery & Custom Reconnect Prompt: Automatically detects hung MCP/Gemini turns past the configured threshold (default 5 min), cancels the stuck turn, protects unsent user drafts, and sends a customizable reconnect prompt.
• Live Connection Wait Timer & One-Click "Resume Now": Real-time wait elapsed display in the floating HUD and Popup, plus an immediate one-click Resume Now button.
• Human-Like Anti-Throttling Pacing: Randomized pauses, chunked typing, natural hover/click jitter, and a 60-second rolling anti-burst governor.
• Circuit Breaker Desktop Alerts & Badge Wiring: End-to-end amber "!!" / red "!" toolbar badges and localized desktop notifications that jump straight to the affected Gemini tab when clicked.
• Never-Sleep MV3 Background Hardening: ~19.5s SW keepalive, 4-min seamless port rotation, fast 8s zombie-port recovery, and Web Lock anti-freeze shield (76/76 automated & E2E tests passing).
