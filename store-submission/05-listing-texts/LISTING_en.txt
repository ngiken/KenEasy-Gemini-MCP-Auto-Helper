KenEasy Gemini MCP Auto Helper is an open-source productivity extension engineered exclusively for Google Gemini (gemini.google.com). 

It automates repetitive MCP (Model Context Protocol) tool permission dialogs and continue action chips, enabling continuous, unattended AI workflows while shielding your account with an industrial-grade 6-layer circuit breaker and multi-window safety guards.

✨ KEY FEATURES (V1.0.0 OFFICIAL RELEASE)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚡ 1. STALL AUTO-RECOVERY, LIVE WAIT TIMER & HUMAN PACING 🆕
• Live connection/wait elapsed timer right inside the floating HUD and Popup.
• Automatically stops stuck MCP/Gemini turns after your timeout (default 5 min) and sends your customizable reconnect prompt (default: "继续，刚才mcp连接断开了") with draft protection and a one-click "Resume Now" button.
• Built-in human-like anti-throttling pacing (natural delays, chunked typing, safe off-center hover clicks, and a 60s anti-burst governor).

⚡ 2. SMART MCP TOOL APPROVAL (ALWAYS ALLOW FIRST)
• Automatically detects and clicks "Always allow / 始终允许 / 常に許可" permission confirmation dialogs.
• Pierces through Material Web Components Shadow DOM, <slot> projections, and role="menu" dropdown items.
• Safe fallback: Optionally falls back to clicking single "Allow once" with strict sibling approval context checks.
• Strict context guard: Never clicks destructive buttons like Deny, Cancel, Delete, or Block, and skips non-chat routes (/gems, /extensions, /settings) and secondary browser restored-tab stale chips.

🖱️ 3. HOLD-TO-DRAG FLOATING HUD
• Left-click and hold the HUD header to position it anywhere across the browser viewport — hovering never traps or sticks the HUD!
• Deferred pointer capture beyond the 4px movement deadzone keeps collapse, theme, and language header buttons immediately clickable.
• Viewport boundary clamping + one-click Reset HUD Position in the popup.

🌗 4. 4-LANGUAGE UI + DARK & LIGHT DUAL MODES
• Instant switching across Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese (UI, notifications, and keywords).
• Synchronized Dark Night and Light Daylight themes across both the floating HUD and popup control panel.

🔔 5. ANTI-STALL & CIRCUIT BREAKER DESKTOP NOTIFICATIONS
• Get instant Windows/macOS native desktop alerts when tool execution or AI response generation stalls longer than your threshold (default 5 minutes), or when a safety circuit breaker trips.
• Browser action icon badge updates to a red "!" (stall / emergency) or amber "!!" (breaker) warning badge.
• 1-Click mute toggle available directly on the HUD and inside the popup settings panel.
• Disabling notifications immediately revokes and clears all pending desktop alerts.
• Clicking the notification brings your Gemini tab into focus instantly.

🤖 6. AUTOMATIC CONTINUE & YES CHIP CLICKING
• Automatically clicks confirmation follow-up chips (such as "↪ Yes") and bottom "Continue" generation buttons.
• Intelligently ignores satisfaction surveys, questionnaires, long prompt chips, and sidebar history.

🛡️ 7. INDUSTRIAL 6-LAYER CIRCUIT BREAKER PROTECTION
Prevents runaway infinite loops, token waste, and accidental quota burnout:
• Layer 1 (Immediate Error Trip): Instantly halts on quota exhaustion, rate limit, or server error cues.
• Layer 2 (Per-Task Limit): Caps consecutive continue clicks per conversation task (default 10).
• Layer 3 (Output Settle Guard): Waits for streaming tokens to settle (1.8s) followed by a debounce cooldown (4s).
• Layer 4 (Daily Continue Limit): Configurable daily continue cap, automatically resets at midnight.
• Layer 5 (Completion Semantics): Pauses on cues like "Task completed" unless explicit truncation occurred.
• Layer 6 (Stagnant & Duplicate Breaker): Computes turn content hashes and trips if output stagnates or loops.

💻 8. NEVER-SLEEP MV3 BACKGROUND OPERATION
• Keeps Gemini active when minimized or running in background tabs through MV3 keepalive heartbeats, 4-minute seamless port rotation, Web Lock anti-freeze shield, and tab discard prevention.
