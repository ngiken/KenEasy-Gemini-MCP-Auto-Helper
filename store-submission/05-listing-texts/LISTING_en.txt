KenEasy Gemini MCP Auto Helper is an open-source productivity extension engineered exclusively for Google Gemini (gemini.google.com).

It automates repetitive MCP (Model Context Protocol) tool permission dialogs and continue action chips, enabling continuous, unattended AI workflows while shielding your account with per-chat controls, isolated emergency stops, background/minimized/sleep click hardening, and an industrial-grade 6-layer circuit breaker.

✨ KEY FEATURES (V2.0)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🛌 1. BACKGROUND, MINIMIZED & SCREEN-OFF / SLEEP CONTINUATION 🆕
• Strengthens background, minimized, and unfocused window detection and triggers `MAIN`-world click escalation on initial clicks for both `Always allow` and `Continue` chips (in addition to retries and stall-recovery send) when hidden, minimized, or unfocused.
• Re-acquires the shared background keepalive Web Lock, rotates/reconnects the Service Worker port, and runs an immediate scan upon returning from hidden, screen-off, or sleep states, paired with `autoDiscardable` per-chat protection and `1200ms` minimize follow-up wakeups.

🎛️ 2. PER-CHAT INDEPENDENT CONTROLS & ISOLATED EMERGENCY STOP
• Toggle 6 core switches independently per Gemini conversation via the HUD ("This chat / Global" switcher) or Popup ("This Conversation" card), with automatic visual markers on customized settings.
• Emergency Stop now halts only the current conversation and persists across SPA navigation while other Gemini conversations and tabs keep running; a separate "Stop All Chats" button is available in the Popup for global halts.
• Automatically migrates `/app` landing-page overrides to newly created `/app/<id>` chats and supports one-click Reset back to global defaults.

⏱️ 3. STALL AUTO-RECOVERY, LIVE WAIT TIMER & HUMAN PACING
• Live connection/wait elapsed timer right inside the floating HUD and Popup.
• Automatically stops stuck MCP/Gemini turns after your timeout (default 5 min) and sends your customizable reconnect prompt (default: "继续，刚才mcp连接断开了") with draft protection and a one-click "Resume Now" button.
• Built-in human-like anti-throttling pacing (natural delays, chunked typing, safe off-center hover clicks, and a 60s anti-burst governor).

⚡ 4. SMART MCP TOOL APPROVAL (ALWAYS ALLOW FIRST)
• Automatically detects and clicks "Always allow / 始终允许 / 常に許可" permission confirmation dialogs.
• Pierces through Material Web Components Shadow DOM, `<slot>` projections, and role="menu" dropdown items.
• Safe fallback: Optionally falls back to clicking single "Allow once" with strict sibling approval context checks.
• Strict context guard: Never clicks destructive buttons like Deny, Cancel, Delete, or Block, and skips non-chat routes (/gems, /extensions, /settings) and secondary browser restored-tab stale chips.

🎨 5. NOTION-STYLE UI & HOLD-TO-DRAG FLOATING HUD
• Rebuilt both HUD and Popup with Notion-inspired neutral styling (Paper Light & Charcoal Dark), 1px hairline borders, subtle hover fills, active drawer highlights, Clear Logs / Refresh Inspect toolbars, and strict zero-`innerHTML` CSP compliance.
• Left-click and hold the HUD header to position it anywhere across the browser viewport — hovering never traps or sticks the HUD!
• Deferred pointer capture beyond the 4px movement deadzone keeps collapse, theme, and language header buttons immediately clickable.

🌗 6. 4-LANGUAGE UI + DARK & LIGHT DUAL MODES
• Instant switching across Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese (UI, notifications, and keywords).
• Synchronized Paper Light and Charcoal Dark themes across both the floating HUD and popup control panel.

🔔 7. ANTI-STALL & CIRCUIT BREAKER DESKTOP NOTIFICATIONS
• Get instant native desktop alerts when tool execution or AI response generation stalls longer than your threshold (default 5 minutes), or when a safety circuit breaker trips.
• Browser action icon badge updates with clear warning states, with 1-click mute on the HUD and popup and instant click-to-focus tab navigation.

🤖 8. AUTOMATIC CONTINUE & YES CHIP CLICKING
• Automatically clicks confirmation follow-up chips (such as "↪ Yes") and bottom "Continue" generation buttons.
• Intelligently ignores satisfaction surveys, questionnaires, long prompt chips, and sidebar history.

🛡️ 9. INDUSTRIAL 6-LAYER CIRCUIT BREAKER & BOUNDARY HARDENING
Prevents runaway infinite loops, token waste, and accidental quota burnout across 116 passing automated & fuzz tests:
• Layer 1 (Immediate Error Trip): Instantly halts on quota exhaustion, rate limit, or server error cues.
• Layer 2 (Per-Task Limit): Caps consecutive continue clicks per conversation task (default 10).
• Layer 3 (Output Settle Guard): Waits for streaming tokens to settle (1.8s) followed by pacing and cooldown guards.
• Layer 4 (Daily Continue Limit): Configurable daily continue cap with Web Locks atomic stats flushing, resetting at midnight.
• Layer 5 (Completion Semantics): Pauses on cues like "Task completed" unless explicit truncation occurred.
• Layer 6 (Stagnant & Duplicate Breaker): Compares turn content hashes and trips if output stagnates or loops, backed by Shadow DOM cycle and clock-skew guards.
